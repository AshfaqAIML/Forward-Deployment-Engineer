What is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

An engineer who works directly with customers: understands their business, models their problems, writes the code and data that solve them, and explains everything in plain language. The academy teaches exactly this — starting from zero.

Listen & model

Turn a customer's story into requirements, numbers and rules — before any code.

Build & ship

Small programs, clean data, working APIs, deployed services — real deliverables at every stage.

Explain & hand over

Documentation, demonstrations and honest limitations — the skills employers actually test.
