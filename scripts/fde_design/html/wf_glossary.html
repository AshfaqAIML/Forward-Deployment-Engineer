break-even Mathematics Business

Simple meaning: the sales level where money in exactly equals money out — no profit, no loss.

Detailed meaning: the quantity at which total revenue equals total cost; computed as fixed cost ÷ (price − variable cost per item). Selling more than this quantity produces profit; selling less produces loss. It assumes prices and costs stay constant over the period.

Example: Amara’s stall fee is 20.00, each roll sells at 4.00 and costs 1.50 to make; each roll contributes 2.50, so she must sell 20 ÷ 2.5 = 8 rolls before earning anything.

Related terms: fixed cost · variable cost · contribution margin · profit
