What is a Forward Deployed Engineer? 2
An engineer who works directly with customers: understands their business, models their problems, writes the code and data that solve them, and explains everything in plain language. The academy teaches exactly this — starting from zero.

Listen & model

Turn a customer’s story into requirements, numbers and rules — before any code.

Build & ship

Small programs, clean data, working APIs, deployed services — real deliverables at every stage.

Explain & hand over

Documentation, demonstrations and honest limitations — the skills employers actually test.

Frequently asked questions 9
Do I need any computer science background? ⌃
No. The programme starts from everyday arithmetic and plain language. Every technical term is explained simply, and the glossary is one click away.
How much time per week? ⌃
The default plan is three 30-minute sessions per week; the dashboard adapts recommendations to your actual pace.
What will I be able to build? ⌃
By Stage 17 you will have deployed small applications, built an AI assistant grounded in a real FAQ, and completed simulated customer engagements with handover documentation.
Are the books and downloads free? ⌃
Every textbook and handbook is readable online and downloadable as DOCX or PDF at no cost.
